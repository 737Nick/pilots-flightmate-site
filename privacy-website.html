Website and Email Privacy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads the App Store badge from Apple. Visiting the site connects your browser to these providers, which may process technical request information, including your IP address, under their own privacy terms. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Apple Privacy Policy.

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to send are received in the developer's Gmail mailbox. They are used to handle your enquiry and reply, and are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose and the related support record. Your email provider and the recipient's provider process the message under their own privacy terms.

The airline suggestion form prepares an email on your device with your airline, any roster system or role you provide, your testing preference, and any additional notes. The form does not submit these details to a form service. Nothing is sent to the developer until you choose Send in your email app. If you send the message, it is used to evaluate possible airline support and contact you about your enquiry or your offer to help test. It is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for that evaluation and follow-up. This does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

Please do not send passwords, login tokens, or personal roster and crew information that is not needed for your enquiry. For questions about your information or a request for access, correction, or deletion, contact aircrewflightmate@gmail.com.
