Privacy Policy for Aircrew FlightMate Android

Last updated: 9 May 2026

Aircrew FlightMate Android is designed to process roster information locally on your device.

Data Collection

Aircrew FlightMate Android does not require users to create an account with Aircrew FlightMate. The app does not operate its own server and does not sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties.

Roster Data

When the user chooses to download or import roster information, the app processes this information locally on the device. Roster data may include duties, flights, standby events, hotel information, comments, and other roster-related details.

This data is used only to display roster information, detect roster changes, merge newly downloaded roster parts, and provide related calendar or duty views inside the app.

Internet Access

The app may use internet access to download roster information from the user’s roster portal when the user chooses to do so. Aircrew FlightMate Android does not send roster data to an Aircrew FlightMate server.

Local Storage

Roster data is stored locally on the user’s device. It remains on the device until the user removes it, clears app data, or uninstalls the app.

Data Deletion

Users can delete local app data by clearing the app data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Third Parties

Aircrew FlightMate Android does not sell user data and does not share roster data with third parties.
